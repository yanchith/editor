When updating the Jai toolchain, be sure to:

- Re-apply our local hotfixes on top of Jai modules. These are usually one-liner modifications directly in modules/Basic or another core module.
  I am trying to mail these in so that the team at Thekla fixes these, but they don't always respond.

- Synchronize our local versions of modules shipping with the compiler with the official ones.
  Usually this means checkin diffs on a per-file basis and either reapplying our changes, or their changes, whichever is quicker.
